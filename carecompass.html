SAMPLE REVIEW
01 · Similar charge — verify

Check whether any repeated service lines represent separate services or a duplicate. Compare dates, codes, and quantities.

02 · Insurance responsibility — confirm

Compare this balance with your EOB, including deductible, copay, coinsurance, and any non-covered amount.

03 · Ask for an itemized statement

Request the detailed bill and confirm unfamiliar codes with the billing department.

These are general review prompts, not confirmed errors. No savings are calculated or guaranteed.
