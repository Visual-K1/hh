Frequently asked questions

Does MedSave really analyse my bill?

Not yet. This prototype does not upload or analyse files. The demo shows sample review prompts only.

Can MedSave guarantee savings?

No. It can help you organise questions, but it cannot guarantee that a charge is incorrect or that a dispute will succeed.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Use the currency selector at the top. The conversion rates in this prototype are illustrative and are not live exchange rates.

Can I pay for Plus right now?

No. The pricing is a concept only. Payment processing and subscription features have not been implemented.

MedSave is a prototype concept, not a healthcare provider, insurer, legal service, or live AI-powered billing auditor. It does not provide medical or legal advice. Do not select real medical documents for this demo. No files are uploaded to a server. Proposed subscription prices and currency conversions are illustrative. A real launch would require appropriate privacy, security, legal, and regulatory review.
