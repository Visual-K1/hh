Questions to consider

01 — Check the itemized charges

Ask for a breakdown of services, dates, quantities, and individual costs.

02 — Confirm insurance adjustments

03 — Ask about unfamiliar items

Request an explanation from the billing department before assuming a charge is incorrect.
